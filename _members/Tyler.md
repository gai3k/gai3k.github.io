Originally from Albuquerque, New Mexico, Tyler earned a Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry from Arizona State University. As an undergraduate researcher in Abhinav Acharya’s lab, he worked on developing novel biopolymer microparticles for drug delivery and immune cell modulation. He also conducted his undergraduate thesis research in Nhan Tran’s lab at the Mayo Clinic, investigating the role of sexual dimorphism in receptor tyrosine kinase function in glioblastoma.
 
Now a predoctoral student studying immunology at the Mayo Clinic, Tyler is interested in how inflammation shapes the glioblastoma tumor microenvironment and how modulating phagocytic cell function influences tumor progression. Outside the lab, he enjoys reading science fiction, experimenting with new recipes, listening to classical music, and rowing with his crew team on Tempe Town Lake.
